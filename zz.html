Roadbound handbook

How the company works

1

Choose local work

A vehicle can accept only a contract collecting from its current depot. Compare estimated net, not only gross pay.

2

Loading, driving, unloading

These are separate timed stages. Payment is released only after unloading finishes at the destination.

3

Plan fuel before empty

Fuel is sold only at mapped service stations. Add a stop while driving; at zero fuel, movement and map progress stop.

4

Vehicles stay where they finish

Take a return load or pay for an empty run. Regional depots improve local job supply and handling speed.

5

Know the three meters

Fuel controls range, condition controls reliability and speed, and focus powers active pace boosts.

6

Grow into a fleet

Buy vehicles, hire one driver per moving vehicle, open depots and unlock automation through company facilities.

7

Read live traffic

Green routes are clear, yellow is light, orange is medium and red is heavy. Busy routes take longer but can include a congestion allowance. Urban and industrial roads also use working traffic lights: vehicles stop at red, prepare on red and amber, and move on green.
