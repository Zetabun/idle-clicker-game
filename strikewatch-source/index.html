WHAT IS STRIKEWATCH?

Strikewatch is a tactical management game. You build and prepare the club; five autonomous operators carry out your decisions in live elimination matches.

1RECRUIT & EQUIPSign operators and give each one a suitable role, weapon and armour setup.
2SET THE PLANChoose the team's formation, tempo, preferred engagement range and priorities.
3WATCH THEM EXECUTEYou spectate. Operators move, aim, take cover, reload and fight for themselves.
4LEARN & IMPROVEUse the debrief to train weaknesses, manage the club and earn promotion to the Pro League.
REAL MATCH FORMATFive operators per team · no respawns during a round · first team to win three rounds wins the match.
